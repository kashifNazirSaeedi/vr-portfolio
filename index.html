About

01 — studio

Blackbox Creations is a one-person Unity studio run out of Lahore, Pakistan. For six years the work has centered on the unglamorous problems that decide whether a game actually ships: draw calls, mesh budgets, and the networking layer nobody wants to touch twice.

That focus turned into a small catalog of Asset Store tools — mesh optimization, LOD generation, and multiplayer scaffolding — used by studios who'd rather buy a solved problem than debug one at 2am. Alongside that, the studio builds VR/XR experiences for Meta Quest and takes on contract work for teams that need a specific systems problem solved well.

Blackbox Creations is a Unity development studio with over 6 years of experience in game development, XR applications, and tool creation.
